Our Services

Every finish we offer is crafted with the same commitment to quality for homeowners in Brampton, Ontario and across the GTA. Whether you prioritize durability, aesthetics, or both, we have the solution for your project.

01

Broom Finish

Most Popular

A broom finish is one of the most common and practical concrete finishes. It involves dragging a broom across freshly poured concrete to create a fine, textured surface that provides excellent traction. Ideal for areas where slip resistance is essential — sidewalks, patios, driveways, and garages.

We also offer colour washes to tint the concrete a hue of your choosing, and decorative border options to define and elevate any space.

  • Cost-effective
  • Slip-resistant
  • Low maintenance
  • Colour washes available
  • Customizable borders
Colour Options Available — Ask us about our full range of wash colours and border styles to personalize your finish.
02

Exposed Aggregate

Natural Look

Exposed aggregate concrete is created by removing the top layer of cement paste to reveal the underlying natural stones. This produces a rich, textured surface with a distinctive appearance that varies based on the aggregate blend selected.

It's perfect for driveways, sidewalks, and patios, enhancing the overall atmosphere and curb appeal of your home. Custom border combinations are available to frame and define the space beautifully.

  • Unique natural texture
  • Durable long-term
  • Customizable borders
  • Improves curb appeal
Note: Exposed aggregate requires periodic sealing to maintain its appearance and protect the surface from the elements.
03

Stamped Finish

Decorative

Stamped concrete is designed to replicate the look of premium materials — brick, slate, flagstone, stone, or tile — at a fraction of the cost. It offers a decorative, highly customizable surface ideal for patios, driveways, and walkways.

With a wide variety of patterns, textures, and colour options, stamped concrete dramatically enhances curb appeal and gives your outdoor space a premium, tailored look. Keep in mind that this finish trades some slip resistance and raw durability for its superior aesthetic qualities.

  • Highly decorative
  • Wide pattern selection
  • Multiple colour options
  • Luxury appearance
Consider: Stamped concrete offers less slip resistance than other finishes. It's best suited for areas with controlled foot traffic or where aesthetics are the priority.
